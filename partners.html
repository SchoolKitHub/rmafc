Partners & institutions · Beijing · 24–25 February 2027

The institutions behind Nigeria's oil and gas investment

In Beijing, investors meet the regulators, the national oil company and the investment and infrastructure bodies that decide how Nigerian projects are licensed, financed and built. This page sets out what each institution does, and the partners confirmed for the summit.

Organised by

Revenue Mobilisation Allocation and Fiscal Commission (RMAFC)

The constitutional body that monitors accruals to and disbursements from the Federation Account, and sets how oil and gas revenue is shared between federal, state and local governments.
